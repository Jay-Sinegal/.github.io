# Content distribution plan

## Measurement convention

Use tagged links whenever a profile, partner, or post links to the site:

`?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=MEDIUM&utm_campaign=ARTICLE_SLUG`

- LinkedIn organic post: `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social`
- Professional profile: `utm_source=PROFILE_NAME&utm_medium=referral`
- Partner or organization: `utm_source=PARTNER_NAME&utm_medium=referral`
- Email sent directly by Jaylen: `utm_source=direct_outreach&utm_medium=email`

Keep source names lowercase and stable. Never place private contact or audience data in UTM parameters.

## LinkedIn publishing sequence

Publish one article at a time, separated by several days. Each post should lead with a useful idea, not merely announce a link.

### Louisiana LLC article

Opening: Forming an LLC is permission to start building—not proof that the business is ready for customers.

Post: Louisiana founders often move quickly from filing documents to logos and promotion. The harder work is separating company operations, defining the customer and offer, choosing a position, building a route to the first customers, and measuring business evidence. I put that sequence into a practical checklist for what comes after formation.

Link: `https://jaylensinegal.com/blog/what-to-do-after-starting-an-llc-in-louisiana.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=llc_louisiana`

### Acadiana community marketing article

Opening: Families do not experience a local business as a keyword. They experience its reputation.

Post: Community marketing works when useful presence and local trust arrive before the sales pitch. For businesses serving Lafayette and Acadiana, that means understanding the household decision, building familiarity early, creating shared value through partnerships, and measuring the path to a real inquiry.

Link: `https://jaylensinegal.com/blog/how-local-businesses-reach-families-in-acadiana.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=acadiana_families`

### Entertainment partnership article

Opening: Attention may open a partnership conversation. Readiness is what makes it credible.

Post: Artists, athletes, events, and entertainment projects need more than an audience count. A clear identity, evidence of audience trust, clean rights, useful proof, shared value, and reliable execution make a brand partnership easier to understand and safer to approve.

Link: `https://jaylensinegal.com/blog/what-makes-an-entertainment-brand-partnership-ready.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=partnership_ready`

## Profile and partnership checklist

- Use the same name, role description, Lafayette/Acadiana context, portrait, and `https://jaylensinegal.com/` link across legitimate professional profiles.
- Add the most relevant article—not every article—to profile featured sections and project pages.
- Ask existing partners to share an article only when it directly helps their audience; provide a tagged link and a two-sentence summary they can adapt.
- Offer local organizations an educational resource, workshop topic, or member article before asking for promotional exposure.
- Record each placement with its live URL, owner, date, campaign tag, and next review date.
- Do not claim memberships, credentials, client work, or affiliations unless they are current and verifiable.

## Weekly decision loop

1. Reconcile Formspree accepted inquiries with GA4 `generate_lead` events.
2. Review GA4 traffic and engagement by source, medium, campaign, and landing page.
3. Review Search Console query/page impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
4. Improve titles and descriptions where impressions rise but CTR is weak.
5. Expand a topic only after a coherent query cluster repeats for at least four weeks.
6. Give the next distribution slot to the article producing qualified engagement or inquiries—not the article with the most impressions alone.
